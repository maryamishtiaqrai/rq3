Datasets used in this study
===========================

1. Main Dataset (MD)
--------------------
The image-level weapon-detection data originate from the Knife and Pistol Detection Dataset:
https://doi.org/10.1049/ipr2.70442

The manuscript applies cleaning, image-level splitting, and training-only balancing before detector training.
The final MD contains 16,308 images. Validation and test subsets remain in their original evaluation distributions.

2. Crop-level Verifier Dataset (CVD)
------------------------------------
The CVD is derived from MD rather than distributed here as a separate external dataset.
Knife and pistol samples are crops from MD ground-truth boxes. Non-weapon samples are target-domain WBF-mined false-positive regions.
Use 1-cvd-construction/create_clip_dataset_from_wbf_pairs.py to reproduce the crop-generation procedure.

Final CVD counts used in the manuscript:
  Train:      knife 3013 | pistol 3013 | non-weapon 3013 | total 9039
  Validation: knife 1448 | pistol 1216 | non-weapon 1124 | total 3788
  Test:       knife 1447 | pistol 1217 | non-weapon 1052 | total 3716
  Overall total: 16,543 crops

3. External zero-shot set (ZS-MMD)
----------------------------------
The external zero-shot evaluation is derived from the Merged Military Dataset used in the cited CC-WBF study.
It contains 1,710 images and 2,323 annotated weapon instances in the evaluation used here.
Handgun annotations are mapped to pistol to match the two-class detector label space.
The external set is used only after validation-stage selection and is not used for training or threshold selection.
